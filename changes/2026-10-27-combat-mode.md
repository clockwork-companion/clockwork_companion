Combat mode: the GM can switch on combat for the whole table. A red band shows the round, who's up and the turn order, and each character in the fight gets one battle view built round an AP dock: anything that costs AP spends it, and hovering it lights what it would cost. Take damage walks you through blocking, parrying, evading or bracing, your armor and your cards; wounds bleed by themselves each round, and daemons, the Toll and the dreamsphere sit right there. "Sheet" in the band takes you back to the tabs.
