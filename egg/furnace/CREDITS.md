# Furnace Below: art and sound credits

Every asset here is released under **CC0** (public domain dedication). Credit isn't required; it's given gladly.
Files were cropped, packed into atlases (`walls.png`, `sprites.png`, `shotgun.png`) and, for sounds, trimmed and converted to mono MP3.

## Art (OpenGameArt.org)
- Wall, door and panel textures: [Old school FPS wall textures](https://opengameart.org/content/old-school-fps-wall-textures), CC0
- Key, treasure, barrels, candles, plant, stove, bones: [Oldschool FPS decoration sprites](https://opengameart.org/node/185391) (same author), CC0
- Shotgun (first person): [FPS weapon sprites](https://opengameart.org/content/fps-weapon-sprites), sprites by ragnar random, derivative of works by Quaternius, CC0
- Close-combat mutant: [Monster close combat mutant for FPS game using sprites](https://opengameart.org/content/monster-close-combat-mutant-for-fps-game-using-sprites) by Nmn, CC0
- Soldier: [Purple sci-fi soldier FPS sprite sheet](https://opengameart.org/content/purple-sci-fi-soldier-fps-sprite-sheet), CC0
- Health and ammo: [Items: armor, health, ammo](https://opengameart.org/content/items-armor-health-ammo) by Nmn, CC0
- Soldier's shot: bullet art from [The Robot (free sprite)](https://opengameart.org/content/the-robot-free-sprite), CC0
